Artificial Intelligence: From Enigma to AGI

About the Project

An interactive HTML presentation that explores the evolution of Artificial Intelligence (AI), from its early beginnings to the future of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI).

#Features

Interactive presentation slides

Animated visual elements

Simple and engaging explanation of AI concepts

Web-based access through a browser

#Technologies Used

HTML

CSS

JavaScript

Tailwind CSS

GSAP

Google Fonts

#Purpose

The purpose of this presentation is to provide an overview of the development of Artificial Intelligence and introduce the concept of Artificial General Intelligence.
