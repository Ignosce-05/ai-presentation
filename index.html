Unlike conventional software that follows rigid programming, AI systems demonstrate the ability to interpret data, recognize patterns, and evolve their responses based on experience.

1940s — THE CRYPTOGRAPHIC WAR

World War II: The Enigma Challenge

The Enigma Machine

A sophisticated electro-mechanical rotor cipher device employed by Nazi Germany to encrypt top-secret military communications with billions of possible combinations.

The Bombe

Developed by British cryptologists at Bletchley Park, this device automated decryption, reducing code-breaking time from years to hours.

Strategic Impact

The successful decryption provided the Allies with unprecedented intelligence, significantly shortening World War II and saving countless lives.

Alan Turing:
Father of Modern Computing

Alan Turing stands as the foundational figure of modern computer science and artificial intelligence. His theoretical conception of the Turing Machine established the mathematical framework for universal computation.

His 1950 paper "Computing Machinery and Intelligence" introduced the Turing Test, establishing the philosophical benchmark for machine intelligence that influences AI research to this day.

The Turing Machine

The Turing Machine serves as the fundamental theoretical model for modern computing, establishing the conceptual boundaries of mechanical computation and providing the mathematical framework for algorithm analysis.

2022 — INARI AGRICULTURE

FloraBERT

When AI Learned to Read the Genetic Language of Plants

🧬 The Model

  • ▸ Trained on 93 plant species + 25 maize varieties
  • ▸ Uses transformer architecture (BERT) to analyze DNA promoter sequences
  • ▸ Predicts gene expression across multiple plant tissues
  • ▸ Cross-species transfer learning with attention-based neural networks

💡 The Breakthrough

FloraBERT treats DNA as language—where promoter sequences are sentences and nucleotides are words.

It can predict how a single mutation changes the "meaning" of a genetic sequence, enabling scientists to "edit" plant traits with unprecedented precision.

Key Innovation:

"A single nucleotide change in a promoter's DNA 'sentence' can alter its biological meaning—FloraBERT predicts this with remarkable accuracy."
